OUR BACKGROUND

A tradition baked with heart.

Est. 1993

A tradition that began in 1993

GEM'S Bakery began in 1993 with a simple passion for baking fresh, delicious bread using traditional methods. What started as a humble bakery grew through the support of the local community and the familiar taste of freshly baked Filipino favorites.

Through the years, we have remained committed to preserving the taste and tradition of local baking. From freshly baked pandesal to other beloved bakery treats, every product reflects our dedication to quality, tradition, and the generations of customers who have supported us.
